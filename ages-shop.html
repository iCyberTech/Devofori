Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

🛍️

Real-time catalog

Products, prices and photos update live as merchants make changes. No refreshing.

🧺

Guest or account

Start shopping instantly. Create an account later and your cart comes with you.

❤️

Saved items

Tap the heart to keep products for later, synced to your account.

🔐

Easy, safe sign-in

Email with a one-time code, Sign in with Apple (iOS) or Google.

🧾

Server-priced orders

Totals are calculated on our servers from current prices, never from the app.

You are in control

Delete your account and data any time from the Me tab.
