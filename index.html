Our apps

Two apps, one marketplace.

Shoppers browse and buy in Ages Shop. Approved merchants run their stock from AgesMate. Every new product is checked by us before shoppers see it; price and stock updates go live within seconds.

Ages Shop

iOS & Android

A beautiful shopping app with real product photos, instant search, saved items and a smooth cart. Browse as a guest, then create an account without losing your cart.

  • Live catalog that updates as merchants edit
  • Sign in with email, Apple or Google
  • Prices in Ghana cedis (GH₵)
Coming soon to the App Store & Google Play

AgesMate

For merchants · iOS & Android

The merchant app. Apply to sell, get approved, then list products with photos and a live preview of exactly how they will look to shoppers.

  • Publish or hide a product in one tap
  • Live preview before you save
  • Every merchant is reviewed and approved
See AgesMate
How it fits together

From a merchant's phone to a shopper's screen.

Apply and get approved

A merchant creates an account in AgesMate and applies. We review the business before anything can be listed.

List with confidence

Add a photo, a price and a few details. A live preview shows the exact Ages Shop card before it is published.

Live for shoppers

The moment it is saved, Ages Shop updates on every phone. Hide it and it disappears just as fast.

What we care about

Details that make it feel premium.

⚡

Native and fast

Built with SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose, so every screen feels immediate and at home on the phone.

🔒

Secure by design

Always in sync

Real-time updates keep shoppers and merchants looking at the same, current catalog.

Built for Ghana
