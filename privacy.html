This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Rockson Richard Ofori, an individual trading as DevOfori ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use Ages Shop (the shopping app) and AgesMate (the app for approved merchants), why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We have written it to be read, not skimmed past. If anything is unclear, contact us at the address below.

1Who is responsible for your information

Rockson Richard Ofori, trading as DevOfori, is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. DevOfori is a trading name of an individual, not a registered company. You can reach us at oforirockson@proton.me, which is our only contact address.

2What we collect

Ages Shop (shoppers).

  • Account details: your email address and name when you create an account, and a password (stored by our authentication provider in hashed form, never in readable form).
  • Sign-in details: if you use Sign in with Apple or Google, the identifier and email address that service shares with us, and your name if you choose to share it.
  • Guest activity: if you browse without an account, we create an anonymous guest identifier so your cart and saved items work.
  • Shopping activity: items in your cart, saved items and your orders (what you bought, prices, totals, dates and status).
  • Delivery details and saved addresses: the name, phone number and address you enter at checkout are saved with your order and shown to the merchant who fulfils it. Addresses you save under Me > Addresses, and the mobile money network and number you set as your default, are kept only on your device. We do not receive them unless you use one to place an order.
  • Your location, only if you choose it: when you tap "Use my current location" while adding a delivery address, the app reads your phone's location once and turns it into an address using the address lookup built into your phone (Apple's on iPhone, the phone maker's or Google's on Android). We use it only to fill in the address form, which you can edit. We do not store your coordinates, track where you go, or read your location in the background.
  • Reports about a seller: if you report a problem with an item, we collect the reason you choose, your description, any photos you attach, the order and item concerned, and the phone number you gave for delivery. A report is private: only we can see it, and it is not shown to the merchant. Photos are kept in private storage.
  • Reviews you write: your star rating, your comment, the date, and your first name and last initial. A review is shown publicly on the product with a "Verified purchase" mark. We never show your email address.

AgesMate (merchants).

  • Your account details as above, plus your business name, your name, phone number, city, a short description of what you sell, and the status of your application.
  • The products you list: titles, descriptions, prices, categories, sizes and the photos you upload.

What we do not collect. We do not currently use advertising trackers, sell personal information, or run third-party analytics inside the apps. The apps do not track your location, and they do not ask for your contacts or microphone. Your location is read only when you tap "Use my current location".

3How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run the shop: show products, keep your cart and saved items, and create and track your orders.
  • To fill in your delivery address from your phone's location, when you ask us to.
  • To review the reports you send about an item or a seller, decide what should happen (for example a return or a refund), and tell you the outcome.
  • To show reviews and work out each product's rating, and to award automatic badges (Sale, Hot, New) from real prices and sales.
  • To process payments through our payment partner when payments are available.
  • To review merchant applications, and let approved merchants manage and publish their products.
  • To send essential messages, such as email confirmation codes and password resets.
  • To keep the apps safe, prevent fraud and abuse, fix problems and meet legal obligations.

We use your information only for these purposes.

4Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the apps, and only what they need:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, authentication and file storage.
  • Brevo: delivers our emails, such as confirmation codes and password resets.
  • Paystack: processes card and mobile money payments. We do not see or store your full card details or mobile money PIN. The card payment screen and your receipt may show Paystack's name.
  • Apple and Google: if you choose to sign in with them. If you use "Use my current location", your phone's operating system may send your location to Apple or Google to look up the address, under their own privacy terms. We do not receive or keep your coordinates.
  • Merchants: a merchant can see the orders for their own products and the delivery details needed to fulfil them. Merchants must use that information only to fulfil your order. Merchants do not see the reports you make about them.

Product information that merchants publish, including photos, is visible to all shoppers by design. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the apps. We do not sell your personal information.

5Where your information is stored

Our providers operate internationally. Our database is hosted by Supabase in the United States, so your information may be processed outside Ghana. We choose providers that protect information to recognised standards and we share only what is necessary.

6How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your account, cart, saved items, reviews and (for shoppers) order history from the apps, and we hide a merchant's products. Where the law requires us to keep certain records, such as transaction records, we keep only what is needed and for as long as required. Backups may hold information for a short additional period before they are overwritten. Reports you send about an item or seller, and their photos, are kept for as long as we need them to resolve the report and keep a record of it.

7Your choices and rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you can object to or restrict some uses, as provided by Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and other laws that apply to you.

  • Location: you can refuse the location permission, or switch it off for the app in your phone's settings, at any time. You can always type your address instead.
  • Delete your account: in either app go to Me (Ages Shop) or Profile (AgesMate) and choose Delete account. This removes your account and data as described above.
  • Anything else: email oforirockson@proton.me and we will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

8Security

We protect information with encryption in transit, access controls that limit each user to their own data, and carefully restricted administrator access. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

9Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Website

Our website, devofori.com, is a simple informational site. It does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make an important change we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the apps. Continuing to use the apps after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12Contact us

Privacy questions or requests: oforirockson@proton.me.
